TWO SEPARATE VERDICTS, and they differ.

REJECTED: the fitted optimum does not align with the multinomial
score.  Its median |cosine| is 0.11 against a chance level of
0.14 in this many dimensions -- indistinguishable from an
arbitrary direction.  Whatever the fitted eigenvector is finding, it
is not the score of a multinomial band model, and that hypothesis is
reported as tested and discarded rather than quietly dropped.

CONFIRMED, and independently of the above: weights derived from a
unit's band profile transfer between bearings at 0.18, which is the
chance level, while entropy's transfer at 0.96.

The reason is visible in the two definitions.  Score weights are
p'/p and depend on the DERIVATIVE of the band profile, which is how
this particular bearing happens to be degrading.  Entropy's weights
are -(1 + log p) and depend on the LEVEL of the profile, which is a
property of the machine type and is shared.  A statistic whose
weights read the level transfers; one whose weights read the
derivative cannot, however optimal it is on the record it was fitted
to.  That is a mechanism for the Section 9 paradox -- fitted linear
rules beat entropy in sample and fail by a factor of 136 when moved --
and it does not depend on the multinomial model that was just
rejected.
