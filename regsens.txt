--------------------------------------------------
         1.00         0.011       0.003     0.31

With the regimes known the correction is essentially complete: the
residual error stays under 0.006 of a lifetime where the
uncorrected error reaches 0.275.  On the turbofan the repair
was only partial, and the difference is that there the regimes had to
be recovered by clustering rather than given -- which locates the
remaining half of that shortfall in the regime assignment, not in the
method.
