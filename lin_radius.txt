measured against predicted degradation of the floor

The floor is built on a robust noise scale and the error follows the
second moment, so on heavy-tailed residuals the two differ by a factor
that is present at every offset including zero.  It is measured as the
standard deviation of the standardised residual and divided out, and the
raw column is kept so nothing is hidden.

The last column is the median of the per-record ratio, not the ratio of
the two medians: C spans two orders of magnitude across these bearings,
so the two are different statistics and only the paired one is a test.

The expansion was expected to fail well before the far end and does not,
so the closed form is not merely a local approximation here.

at delta = 0 the measured error is 1.01 times the floor,
which is the attainment of Section 5 recovered in this setting.

The radius scales as G^(-1/4), and Section 6.5 measures that an
estimator which must also fit the trend holds only 1.32e-05 of the
nominal information at the exponent these records show.  The radius
that applies to such an estimator is therefore 17 times larger,
0.072 of a lifetime rather than 0.0043.  The oracle figure is
the conservative one and both are reported.
