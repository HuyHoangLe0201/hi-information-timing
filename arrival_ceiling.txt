Places where the prose points at a later section, at the last run that was
accepted.  All four were read once, and each is a promise whose sentence stands
on its own; none asks the reader to hold an undefined thing in mind:

  sec:reading        "the curve is estimated from the record rather than fitted
                     to a parametric family, which matters because measured
                     degradation shapes do not share one" -- the claim is made,
                     the later section supplies the measurement behind it;
  sec:distribution   the serial correlation is measured on ten indicators, and
                     the sentence now says what they are before naming where
                     they are compared;
  sec:bayes          the re-anchoring condition, whose numbers are given in
                     place;
  sec:mechanism      "the mechanism is taken up in Section ..." -- an
                     announcement, carrying nothing the reader needs yet.

Raise this only after reading the printed list.  A rise means the reader is
being asked to wait for something one more time, which is the thing this layer
exists to keep from growing quietly.  It was written after five places were
found where a term, a set of indicators or a proposition arrived before what it
carried: the rank guard, named in the discussion and defined nowhere in the main
text; "information-weighted", used in Section 4 and defined in Appendix A; a
correlation measured over indicators three sections early; and three
propositions invoked by number alone.
