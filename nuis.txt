At the exponent these records show, 8.3, the nuisance rung alone costs
a factor 275 in error.  Section 6 measures the whole oracle-to-ridge gap
at 34.  The nuisance term therefore does not merely contribute to that
gap, it exceeds it, which says the two are not
measured on the same quantity: Section 6 sweeps a fixed synthetic trend
shape while beta = 8.3 is read off real bearings.  The honest statement
is that the nuisance cost is large enough to account for a gap of that
size, not that it has been shown to be its cause.
